Whose Expectations Matter?

The world is complex.

We each live in a bubble of expectations, traditions, and unspoken rules that are designed to keep us safe.

These guardrails are helpful, but restrictive. And as the world speeds up, restriction will only get riskier.

How to get the benefits, without the cost?

Expectations exist for a reason.

They let us inherit other people's hard-won lessons, instead of learning them the slow, painful way.

But even the hardest-won lessons are flawed. They're cookie-cutter solutions to unique problems. So in the wrong context, they become actively misleading.

How do we know what to trust, then?

Nothing is certain.

It's our job, as individuals, to figure out what we think is important, and act accordingly.

This is a scary thing to realise. It's tempting to shy away from it by doubling down on our old lives, but that only makes things worse.

Like it or not, we're only ever conforming to our own expectations.
